[The Author's Arrival in Virginia.]

I shall say nothing of it, nor shall I detain the Rea- der with the story of my own affairs, and the trou- bles that befel me during about 13 months of my stay there; but in the next Chapter enter imme- diately upon my second Voyage into the South Seas, and round the Globe. CHAP.
