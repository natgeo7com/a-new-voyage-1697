I was at that time a hunting with the General for Beef, which he had a long time promifed us. But now I faw that there was no credit to he gi- ven to his word; for I was a week out with him and faw but four Cows, which were fo wild, that we did not get one. But I told him the truth, and advifed him not to be too credulous of the Generals promifes.
