Old Panama. The New City. The great Concourse there from Lima and Por- tobel, &c. upon the Arrival of the Spanish Armada in the West Indies. The Course the Armada takes : with an incidental Account of the first Inducements that made the Privateers undertake the passage over the Isthmus of Da- rien into the South Seas, and of the particular beginning of their Correspondence with the Indi- ans that inhabit that Isthmus.
