They grow generally where Plantains do, being fet intermixt with them purpofely in their Plantain-walks. They have plenty of Clove- bark, of which I faw a Ship-load, and as for Cloves, Raja Laut, whom I fhall have occafion to mention, told me, that if the Englifh would fettle there, they could order matters fo in a little time, as to fend a Ship load of Cloves from thence every year. I have been informed that they grow on the boughs of a Tree about as big as a Plumb-tree, but I ne- ver happened to fee any of them.
