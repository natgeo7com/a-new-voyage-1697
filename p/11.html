While they are among the English, they wear good Cloaths, and take delight to go neat and tight; but when they return again to their own Country, they put by all their Cloaths, and go after their own Country fashion, wearing only a small piece of Linnen tyed about their waistes, hanging down to their knees.

C H A P. II.

The Author's Land Journey from the South to the North-Sea, over the Terra-Firma, or Isthmus of Darien.

BEing landed, May the 1st, we began our march about 3 a Clock in the Afternoon, directing our course by our Pocket Compasses N. E. and having gone about 2 miles, we came to the foot of a hill where we built small Hutts and lay all night ; having excessive Rains till 12 a Clock.
