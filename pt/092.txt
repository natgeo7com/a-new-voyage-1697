The Management of their Sick.   

We remained at John Fernando's $16$ days ; our sick
men were ashore all the time, and one of Captain
Eaton's Doctors, (for he had $4$ in his Ship) tending
and feeding them with Goat, and several Herbs,
whereof here is plenty growing in the Brooks ; and
their Diseases were chiefly Scorbutick.   

CHAP.
