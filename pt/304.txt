Their Provisions for their Voyage.

Brafs Clock, an Astralobe, and a large Telescope:
for which Present the Frier sent us aboard 6 Hogs,
and a roasting Pig, 3 or 4 Bushels of Potatoes, and
50 l. of Manila Tobacco. Then we prepared to be
gone, being pretty well furnished with Provision to
carry us to Mindanao, where we designed next to
touch. We took aboard us as many Coco-nuts as we
could well stow, and we had a good stock of Rice,
and about 50 Hogs in Salt.

CHAP.
