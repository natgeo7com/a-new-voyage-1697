Boy lately taken) ſuffered him quietly to land, and
gathered all about to hear how it was with him. He
told them particularly, that he was one of their
Countrymen, and how he had been taken many
years ago by the Engliſh, who had uſed him very
kindly ; that they were miſtaken in being ſo much

for this he had learnt of the Youth, and his Father
