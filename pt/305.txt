They resolve to go to Mindanao. Their depar-
ture from Guam. Of the Philippine Islands.
The Isle Luconia, and its chief Town and
Port, Manilo, Manila, or Manilbo. Of the
rich Trade we might establish with these Islands.
St. Johns Island. They arrive at Mindanao.
The Island described. Its Fertility. The Lib-
by Trees, and the Sago made of them. The
Plantain Tree, Fruit, Liquor, and Cloth. A
smaller Plantain at Mindanao. The Bonano.
Of the Clove-bark, Cloves and Nutmegs, and
the Methods taken by the Dutch to monopo-
lize the Spices. The Betel-nut, and Arek tree.
The Durian, and the Jaca-tree and Fruit. The
Beasts of Mindanao. Centapes or Forty
Legs, a venomous Insect, and others. Their
Fowls, Fish, &c. The Temperature of the
Climate, with the Course of the Winds, Torna-
does, Rain, and Temper of the Air through-
out the year.
