I might have given a further account of several
things relating to this Country; the In-land parts of
which are so little known to the Europeans. But I
shall leave this province to Mr. Wafer, who made a
longer abode in it than I; and is better able to do
it than any man that I know; and is now preparing
a particular Description of this Country for the
Press.
