The evening of this 18th day was very dismal.
The Sky looked very black, being covered with
dark Clouds, the Wind blew hard, and the Seas
ran high. The Sea was already roaring in a
white fome about us ; a dark night coming on,and
no Land in sight to shelter us, and our little Ark
in danger to be swallowed by every Wave; and
what was worst of all, none of us thought our
selves prepared for another World. The Reader
may better guess, than I can express, the confusi-
on that we were all in. I had been in many
eminent dangers before now, some of which I
have already related, but the worst of them all
was but a play-game, in comparison with this.
