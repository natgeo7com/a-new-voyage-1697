day more or leſs. One of our men in the midſt
of theſe hardſhips was found guilty of theft, and
condemned for the ſame, to have 3 blows from
each man in the Ship, with a 2 inch and a half
rope on his bare back. Captain Swan began firſt,
and ſtruck with a good will, whoſe example was
followed by all of us.

It was very ſtrange that in all this Voyage we
did not ſee one Fiſh, not ſo much as a Flying-fiſh,
nor any ſort of Fowl, but at one time, when we
were by my account 4975 miles Weſt from Cape
Corrientes, then we ſaw a great number of Boobies,
which we ſuppoſed came from ſome Rocks not far
from us, which were mentioned in ſome of our
Sea-carts, but we did not ſee them.

After we had run the 1900 leagues by our reck-
oning which made the Engliſh account to Guam,
the men began to murmur againſt Captain Swan,
for perſwading them to come this Voyage ; but he
gave them fair words, and told them that the Spa-
niſh account might probably be the trueſt, and ſee-
ing the gale was likely to continue, a ſhort time
longer would end our troubles.
