// sub:
The Author's departure from Bencouli, on board
the Defence, under Captain Heath. Of a
fight between some French Men of War from
Ponticheri, and some Dutch Ships from
Pallacat, joined with some English, in fight
of Fort St. George. Of the bad Water taken
in at Bencouli ; and the strange sickness and
death of the Seamen, supposed to be occasioned
thereby. A Spring at Bencouli recommended.
The great exigences on board: a Consult held,
and a Proposal made to go to Johanna. A
Resolution taken to prosecute their Voyage to
the Cape of Good Hope. The Wind favours
them. The Captains Conduct They arrive at
the Cape, and are helped into Harbour by the
Dutch. A description of the Cape, its Prof-
pect, Soundings, Table Mount, Harbour,
Soil, &c. large Pomegranates and good Wines.
The Land-Animals. A very beautiful kind
of Onager, or Wild Ass, striped regularly
black and white. Ostrages. Fish. Seals.
The Dutch Fort and Factory. Their fine
Garden. The Traffick here.
