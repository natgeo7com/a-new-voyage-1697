⚓️ Intro of W. Dampier, & why it's necessary

I shouldn't have to introduce W. Dampier, but since the whole world knows Robinson Crusoe but not Old Dampier, it's necessary to put in some context first.

This man is an adventurer, pirate, scientist, travel-cum-scientific writer, the first Englishman who circumnavigated the world 3 times.

He was there in the Golden Age of Sailing, fighting and barbecuing with legendary pirate captains in the Caribbeans.

he was also there in the very beginning of Scientific Thinking and Writing.

His book A New Voyage Round the World was a massive sensation that fundamentally shaped the genre of travel writing and inspired generations of explorers. This man put piracy and science, the coolest things, in one book. He more than directly inspired Daniel Defoe, Charles Darwin, ..., his books was required reading in the Royal Navy for its maritime knowledge and accuracy.

It's safe to say the Golden Age of Inquiry is also the Golden Age of Sailing. Obviously adventure and commerce are the best teachers. England was a very open-minded country at that time, and Dampier was most celebrated as well. But since then, he basically disappeared in public education. We are still very open, but the world is not free market any more.
